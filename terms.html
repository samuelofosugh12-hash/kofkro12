1 About these terms

Kofkro LMIS is a platform that connects job seekers, young people, employers and training providers in the Kofkro community. By creating an account or using the platform, you agree to these Terms & Conditions.

If you do not agree with any part of these terms, please do not create an account or use the platform.

2 Who can use Kofkro LMIS

Kofkro LMIS is open to individuals, businesses and organisations who want to find work, offer work or share training in the community.

  • You must give true information when you sign up.
  • If you are under 18, please use the platform with the knowledge of a parent or guardian.
  • If you register for an organisation, you confirm that you are allowed to act on its behalf.

3 Your account

When you create an account you choose a role: job seeker, employer or training provider. You are responsible for everything that happens under your account.

  • Keep your email address and details up to date.
  • Choose a strong password of at least 8 characters and keep it private.
  • Do not share your account or use someone else's account.
  • Tell us straight away if you think someone else is using your account.

4 Job seekers

If you use Kofkro LMIS to look for work, you agree to:

  • Give accurate information about your skills, experience and qualifications.
  • Apply only for opportunities you are genuinely interested in.
  • Treat employers and other users with respect.

5 Employers and organisations

If you post vacancies or opportunities, you agree to:

  • Only advertise real opportunities that you are able to offer.
  • Describe each role honestly, including what the work involves and who to contact.
  • Treat applicants fairly and not discriminate unlawfully.
  • Never ask applicants to pay money to be considered for, or to receive, a job.
  • Follow the labour and employment laws that apply to you.

6 Training providers

If you share courses or training, you agree to:

  • Give accurate details about what you offer, including costs and requirements.
  • Deliver the training in the way you describe it.
  • Not make promises about jobs or results that you cannot keep.

7 Acceptable use

You must not use Kofkro LMIS to:

  • Post false, misleading or fraudulent information.
  • Pretend to be another person, business or organisation.
  • Harass, threaten or discriminate against anyone.
  • Share unlawful, offensive or harmful content.
  • Collect other users' details for purposes unrelated to work or training.
  • Try to damage, interrupt or gain unauthorised access to the platform.

8 Content you share

You keep ownership of the information you add to Kofkro LMIS, such as your profile, vacancies and training details. By adding it, you allow us to display it on the platform so that it can be seen by the people it is meant for.

You are responsible for what you post. We may remove content that breaks these terms or that we believe could cause harm.

9 Your information

To run the platform we collect the details you give us, such as your name, email address, role and any profile, vacancy or training information you add. We use this information to operate your account, to connect job seekers with opportunities, and to keep the platform working properly.

  • Only share information you are comfortable having on your profile or listing.
  • Do not post sensitive personal details about other people without their permission.
  • We handle personal information in line with the data protection laws that apply in Ghana.

10 No guarantees

Kofkro LMIS helps people find each other, but we do not guarantee that you will find a job, a worker or a training place. We also cannot check every listing or profile.

Stay safe: check who you are dealing with before you share personal documents, and never pay money to secure a job.

11 Suspension and ending

We may suspend or close an account if these terms are broken, if information is false, or if the account is used in a way that could harm others or the platform. You can stop using Kofkro LMIS at any time.

12 Changes to these terms

We may update these terms from time to time as the platform grows. The date at the top of this page shows when they were last changed. If you keep using Kofkro LMIS after a change, you agree to the updated terms.

13 Contact us

If you have a question about these terms, or you want to report a problem with a listing or an account, please contact the Kofkro LMIS administrator.
