Who we are

A labour market platform built for our community.

Kofkro LMIS (Labour Market Information System) brings job seekers, young people, employers and training providers together in one place.

Finding the right job, or the right worker, often comes down to knowing what is available close to home. We make that information easy to see and easy to act on.

  • One simple platform for work, skills and people
  • Built around the Kofkro community
  • Create your account for free
Made for Kofkro Local people, local opportunities.
What drives us

Our mission, vision and approach.

Everything on the platform is designed to help people and businesses in Kofkro reach each other.

01

Our mission

To make it easier for people in Kofkro to find work, build their skills and connect with employers.

02

Our vision

A community where everyone can see, and reach, the opportunities that are available around them.

Our approach

Simple, local and open to everyone, from first-time job seekers to established employers.

How it works

Getting started takes three steps.

Connect and grow
